XI & XII Science + MHT-CET Coaching in Thane

Achieve academic excellence in your HSC/CBSE Science boards and secure top scores in the Maharashtra Common Entrance Test (MHT-CET).

Integrated Board & Entrance Preparation at Manpada

Succeeding in the highly competitive 11th and 12th science streams requires a balanced approach that covers both descriptive school board exams and fast-paced entrance exams. Located at Manpada Naka in Thane West, YSR Academy provides a meticulously structured environment with top-tier faculty, detailed chapter sheets, and targeted MHT-CET coaching.

Key Aspects of our Science & CET Program

  • Rigorous Concept Building: Focus on foundational physics laws, chemical configurations, and mathematical formulas to bridge the transition from high school.
  • Subject-Specific Masterclasses: In-depth lectures covering Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology (PCMB) aligned with the latest syllabus.
  • MHT-CET Focus & Speed Tactics: Specialized sessions dedicated to shortcut techniques, time-saving strategies, and systematic problem solving.
  • Modern Practice Test Series: Weekly mock examinations designed according to the MHT-CET online test pattern with complete analytical review.
  • AC Infrastructure & Library Access: Access to a quiet, fully air-conditioned library on Tikuji Ni Wadi Road to maximize self-study hours.

Interactive Course Overview

Our curriculum perfectly aligns State Board HSC textbooks and CBSE modules with the entrance requirements of MHT-CET. Daily doubt-solving sessions, combined with revision tests, ensure that concepts are locked in for both board assessments and entrance rankings.

Science + MHT-CET FAQs

Does YSR Academy cover both boards and MHT-CET syllabus for 11th and 12th Science?

Yes, our program is structured to cover 100% of the Maharashtra State Board (HSC) and CBSE Science curriculum, while concurrently conducting dedicated entrance classes and objective test series for MHT-CET.

Which subjects are taught in the XI & XII Science program at the Thane center?

We provide extensive coaching in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology (PCMB), matching both core school board formats and objective entrance requirements.

How does YSR Academy assist with board examinations practical prep?

We offer conceptual guidance for laboratory sessions, comprehensive viva preparation, and practice assessments for internal board practicals.
