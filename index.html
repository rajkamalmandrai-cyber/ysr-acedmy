Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about YSR Academy, our courses, location, and coaching structure in Thane.

Which is the best coaching institute for JEE and NEET in Thane?

YSR Academy in Manpada, Thane is recognized as a leading coaching institute for JEE (Main & Advanced) and NEET, offering experienced faculty, personal doubt-solving mentorship, weekly test series, and an AC study library.

What coaching courses are offered at YSR Academy Thane?

YSR Academy Thane provides coaching for IIT-JEE (Main + Advanced), Medical NEET, XI & XII Science + MHT-CET, 8th–10th Foundation batches, Maharashtra State Board (SSC), and CBSE curriculum.

Where is YSR Academy located in Thane?

YSR Academy is located at Tikuji Ni Wadi Rd, above Aaryan Ceramic, opposite Metropol hospital, Manpada Naka, Manpada, Thane West, Maharashtra 400607.

Does YSR Academy provide personalized doubt solving and mock test series?

Yes, YSR Academy offers 1-on-1 instant doubt solving, weekly test series patterned after actual JEE/NEET/MHT-CET exams, detailed performance analysis, and dedicated AC library facilities.
