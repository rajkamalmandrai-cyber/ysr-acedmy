CBSE Curriculum Coaching in Thane

Comprehensive NCERT-aligned academic guidance helping CBSE students secure top positions in school and board exams.

Rigorous NCERT Concept Coaching at Manpada

Excellence in the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) curriculum requires a deep understanding of core subjects, structured study methods, and persistent revision. Based in Manpada, Thane West, YSR Academy offers targeted CBSE classes focused on building strong analytical reasoning in Mathematics and Science.

Key Elements of our CBSE Program

  • NCERT-Focused Syllabus: Detailed study of NCERT syllabus guidelines to ensure comprehensive preparation for school assessments and CBSE board exams.
  • Concept-Driven Math & Science Support: Specialized classes for Class 10 board students to simplify complex physics calculations, chemical reactions, and mathematical proofs.
  • Active Assessment Series: Regular weekly chapter reviews and comprehensive syllabus mock exams patterned after real CBSE boards.
  • Small Batch Sizes & Direct Mentoring: Small batch sizes to ensure personal doubt clearance and academic feedback.
  • Safe AC Classrooms & Study Area: Clean, quiet, and air-conditioned classrooms on Tikuji Ni Wadi Road for distraction-free revision.

Aligned for Academic and Competitive Success

Our structured program aligns board expectations with competitive testing techniques. We bridge school lessons with advanced logical reasoning, helping students score higher in board exams and laying down a clear runway for future IIT-JEE and Medical NEET preparation.

CBSE Coaching FAQs

Is YSR Academy's curriculum aligned with the latest CBSE guidelines?

Yes, our academic modules, chapter tests, and worksheets strictly follow the NCERT textbooks and the newest evaluation patterns announced by the CBSE board.

Which classes are covered under the CBSE program in Thane?

We provide specialized coaching classes for secondary CBSE batches (classes 8th to 10th) as well as higher secondary science sections.

Does the academy provide separate doubt solving sessions for CBSE board exams?

Yes, we conduct targeted 1-on-1 sessions where senior teachers resolve doubts, assess sample board answer sheets, and guide students on board exam management.
