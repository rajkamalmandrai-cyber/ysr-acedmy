NEET Coaching in Thane West

Empowering medical aspirants in Thane West with NCERT conceptual clarity, personal mentoring, and mock test schedules.

Expert Medical Entrance Coaching at Manpada

Securing a top rank in the NEET medical entrance exam requires consistent academic effort and clear conceptual logic. At YSR Academy, we provide highly experienced teachers, personal guidance, and detailed doubt-solving modules to help students build a solid foundation.

Our Key Highlights

  • NCERT-Focused Syllabus: Strict adherence to NCERT textbooks, which form the primary reference for NEET questions in Biology, Chemistry, and Physics.
  • Individual Mentorship: Expert counseling to resolve career choices and optimize learning strategy.
  • AC Classrooms & Dedicated Study Library: Safe and quiet environment for self-study and deep preparation.
  • Speed and Accuracy Tests: Structured test sessions to master time management and avoid negative marking.
  • Thorough Study Guides: Detailed physics problem sheets, inorganic chemistry mechanisms, and biological summaries.

Integrated Approach

Our program integrates board syllabus requirements with competitive test formats, helping students maintain top academic scores in their HSC/CBSE boards while building competitive speed for NEET.

NEET Coaching FAQs

Does YSR Academy offer daily practice papers for NEET preparation in Thane?

Yes, we provide systematic study material, including topic-wise Daily Practice Papers (DPPs), question banks, and NCERT-based chapter wise testing to ensure high speed and accuracy.

What is the location of the NEET classes in Thane?

YSR Academy is located in Manpada, Thane West on Tikuji Ni Wadi Road, above Aaryan Ceramic, opposite Metropol hospital. It's easily accessible for students from Ghodbunder Road.
