Maharashtra State Board (SSC) Classes in Thane

Exemplary academic coaching designed to help class 10 secondary students excel in Maharashtra State Board examinations.

Maximize your Class 10 Board Percentage at Manpada

The Maharashtra Secondary School Certificate (SSC) board exam is a key academic milestone for secondary students. At YSR Academy, based in Manpada, Thane, we provide comprehensive, subject-oriented classes focused on textbook conceptual clarity, structured mock board tests, and answering tactics.

Core Highlights of our SSC Board Program

  • Complete Syllabus Support: In-depth classes covering Mathematics (Algebra & Geometry), Science (Part 1 & Part 2), Social Studies, and English language.
  • Textbook Mastery: Deep coverage of Balbharati books to align completely with board evaluation patterns.
  • Board Answer-Writing Practice: Expert-led writing workshops focusing on presentation, step-wise marks, and neat diagrams.
  • Graded Practice Exams: Systematic chapter-wise assessments, unit tests, and three rounds of full board prelims.
  • Modern Study Atmosphere: Peaceful, air-conditioned classrooms on Tikuji Ni Wadi Road, with personal attention for every student.

Experienced Board Mentoring

Our class structures are designed to take the stress out of board preparation. By focusing on fundamental concepts, regular performance reviews, and immediate doubt clearance, we ensure that students build confidence and achieve exceptional final board marks.

State Board (SSC) FAQs

Does YSR Academy cover all class 10 SSC subjects?

Yes, we provide extensive academic lectures, testing, and study materials for Class 10 Maharashtra State Board subjects including Mathematics (Algebra & Geometry), Science (Part 1 & 2), English, and Social Sciences.

What is the study material provided for Maharashtra Board students?

We provide custom chapter summaries, past board papers, mock board test series, and special writing practice sessions tailored to state board evaluation formats.

How does the academy track student performance throughout the year?

We conduct weekly topic tests and share detailed results with parents, alongside holding regular parent-teacher reviews to ensure consistent academic progress.
